Background & Philosophy

I'm a passionate Computer Science student at The Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda, with a strong interest in full-stack development, software engineering, and cybersecurity. I enjoy transforming creative ideas into practical digital solutions — from developing interactive web applications to building intelligent systems.

TRAFIX — Smart Traffic Control Simulator

A traffic control simulator that dynamically adjusts signal timings based on live vehicle density. Instead of fixed timers, the system prioritises roads carrying more traffic — optimising flow and cutting unnecessary waiting at red lights. Built as a desktop GUI to make the algorithm visible and testable, not just describable.
